06 / Scrum Diaries

Lessons from the delivery floor.

Short, practical notes on Scrum, team collaboration and getting delivery unstuck.

Make impediments actionable

Describe the impact, identify the decision or support needed, name an owner and agree a follow-up. Escalation works best when it brings options—not just problems.

Metrics are signals, not scorecards

Use predictability, cycle time, throughput and defect trends to understand the system. Look for bottlenecks and experiments instead of blaming individuals.

PI Planning starts before the event

Check feature readiness, clarify business intent, review dependencies and capacity, and use team explain-backs to catch misunderstandings early.

Keep retrospectives practical

Choose one improvement that the team can test, assign an owner and revisit the outcome next sprint. Small changes with follow-through build trust.
